Art

Questions about humans and AI

A side project I started in September 2026. I use physics, code and AI to put a few questions I keep coming back to into pictures and sound: when AI can find the optimal answer and draw the perfect line, what is left that is ours? Each piece is one of those questions.

Works play as you scroll · turn the sound on · each work notes what the math, the AI and I did.

2026.10.01Ballpoint pen · GPT colour · animation

Off-Brand

In a class on branding, whose brand was I drawing?

Drawn in the margins of a class on branding.

They taught the brand.
I drew mine.

How it was made

A doodle in ballpoint and copper pen, drawn by hand while sitting in a class on branding, signed and dated on the page. Colour was added with ChatGPT (GPT-6 Astra), keeping every pen line, the signature and the date. The animation is code written with Claude (Anthropic): it gently bends parts of the page (the man sways and nods, his spiral eyes turn, the sunflower floats) without redrawing a single line, plus a slight hand-drawn shimmer.

2026.09.25Python · 2D wave simulation · sound

J — Light & Wave

How far does a thought travel before it fades?

My signature, turned into an antenna.

Thoughts merge, grow, vanish.
The farther they travel, the more they fade.

How it was made

About 240 tiny wave sources were placed along the strokes of my signature, a quarter wavelength apart, so each stroke radiates like a traveling-wave antenna. Their cylindrical waves were summed pixel by pixel: gold marks the crests, blue the troughs, and brightness the field strength. The loop is one full wave cycle. Physics set the interference and the nulls; I chose the color, the glow and the fade.

2026.09.26Python · 3D rendering · sound

Undone

If AI can make every line perfect, is the tremble what makes it human?

The perfect line was drawn by an AI.
The trembling one is my hand.

It keeps trying to become perfect.
It keeps coming back to me.

How it was made

The trembling line is traced from my real handwritten signature. An AI smoothed it into perfect curves, then let it fall back to my hand. The melody is read off the signature itself: the higher the pen, the higher the note — clean when perfecting, lo-fi when returning.

2026.09.26Python · optimization · 3D · sound

Argmin

AI finds the same optimum for everyone. What is the worth of a path no one else walks?

2,400 AIs. One answer.
One hand. One path.

The optimum is the same for everyone.
The way there never is.

How it was made

Every particle runs real gradient descent on the landscape, and they all find the same minimum. The golden line is my handwritten signature, which ignores the slope. The particles’ tones collapse into one note as they converge.

2026.09.28Photomosaic optimization · video · sound

Coexistence City

Can a human and a machine build one city without either being erased?

Not a single pixel added.

One human. One machine.
5,184 pieces, each used exactly once, rebuilt into a city.

How it was made

The human is me; the robot was imagined by an AI (ChatGPT). Both portraits were cut into tiles, and an optimizer decided where every tile goes — no copies, no recoloring, nothing left over. Every tile that lands plays a note: soft strings for the human, small metallic pings for the machine.

2026.09.30Flag mosaic · loop · sound

Every Four Years

When every nation is whole on its own, what is left between us?

The lanes were always there.
One day, they became borders.

No one left. Every flag came back whole.
Only the stadium is gone.

How it was made

The flags of 193 UN member states and 2 observer states, each cut into 108 pieces — 21,060 in all, every piece used exactly once, nothing added or recolored. Arranged by color, they become a stadium seen from above. It tears along the lane lines, each piece flies home to its own flag in parade order, and then they gather again, in a seamless 36-second loop. The crowd is heard only while the stadium is whole; the glassy sparkle follows how many nations are in motion. Made in dialogue with Claude (Anthropic), which wrote the layout, rendering and sound code. Flag artwork: flag-icons (MIT).
