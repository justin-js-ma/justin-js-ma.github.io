Research

From molecular-level material design to antennas in real devices.

Full research overview →

My work turns liquid crystals from display materials into field-programmable media for electronics and photonics, and now applies that electromagnetic intuition — together with AI/ML — to antenna engineering for mobile platforms.

2026 — now

AI-Assisted Antenna Engineering

Applying AI/ML to antenna design-space exploration, electromagnetic performance prediction and optimization for mmWave, UWB and satellite links in next-generation mobile and emerging form-factor devices.

NowAdvanced Antenna Lab, Samsung Electronics MX
2020 — 2026

Liquid Crystals for Microwave & mmWave

Tunable phase shifters, filters, phased arrays and reconfigurable intelligent surfaces built on LC dielectrics — targeting low loss, fast switching and beam control for 5G, satellite and 6G.

ResultLC mixture ZOC-A019XX commercialized with JNC (tan δ ≈ 0.006, tunability ≈ 0.33 @ 28 GHz)
2023 — 2026

Printed LC Optoelectronics

Drop-on-demand printing of LC droplets and pixels for smart windows, reflective displays and AR optics — controlling transparency, color and polarization with minimal material and power.

ResultImage-integrated electrochromic PDLC smart windows — Adv. Opt. Mater., Hot Topic
2023 — 2026

Reconfigurable Photonics & Patterning

Laser-assisted and hybrid patterning from millimeters to microns, and topological LC structures, toward programmable metasurfaces and adaptive photonic hardware.

ResultMetastable topological domains locked in Pi cells — Adv. Funct. Mater., Editor’s Choice
